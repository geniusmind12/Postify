Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This page describes the privacy practices of postify ("the Tool"), a small personal tool built by Timileyin to post his own GTA VI clips to his own TikTok account (@leonidaclipstm).

What the Tool collects

The Tool stores, on the device that runs it: the TikTok client key and client secret of your own developer app, and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when you authorize your own account. No other personal data is collected, and nothing is sent to any third party except TikTok's own API (open.tiktokapis.com) when you ask it to post a video.

What the Tool does not collect

It does not collect data about anyone else. It does not read your TikTok followers, messages, or anyone else's videos. It does not sell, share, or rent any data. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking in the Tool.

Your videos

Videos you post through the Tool are uploaded to TikTok under your account and are governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Deleting your data

Delete the Tool's config.json file and all tokens are gone. You can also revoke the Tool's access at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).
